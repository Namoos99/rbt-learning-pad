RBT Learning Pad is an independent study companion. It is not a 40-hour training, a competency assessment, or a substitute for supervision, and it is offered independent of the BACB.

Content is aligned to the RBT Test Content Outline (3rd ed.), the RBT 2026 40-Hour Training Requirements, and the RBT Ethics Code (2.0). Requirements change: confirm details at bacb.com/rbt and with your supervisor. More links are in Resources.
